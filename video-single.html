Admin January 1, 2026

One Piece is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It has been serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine since July 22, 1997, with the chapters collected into 99 tankōbon volumes as of June 2021. The story follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy, a young boy whose body acquired the properties of a pirate and who swears to make the One Piece, the greatest treasure in the world.
